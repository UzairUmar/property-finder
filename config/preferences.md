# My rental preferences

## Budget (hard limits, already enforced by the search)
- 1 bedroom: max S$1,600/month. Either a whole 1-bed unit or a master room with its own attached/private bathroom.
- 2 bedroom: max S$3,200/month.

## Location
- Avoid the far east (Tampines, Pasir Ris, Changi) and far west (Jurong, Boon Lay, Tuas, Choa Chu Kang, Bukit Panjang, Tengah).
- Avoid Ang Mo Kio, Yishun, and Woodlands.
- Central and fringe-central areas preferred.

## Must-haves
- For rooms: private bathroom. A common room with a shared bathroom is a "skip".

## Nice-to-haves
- Close to an MRT station (under 10 min walk).
- Fewer other tenants in the unit is better for room rentals.
